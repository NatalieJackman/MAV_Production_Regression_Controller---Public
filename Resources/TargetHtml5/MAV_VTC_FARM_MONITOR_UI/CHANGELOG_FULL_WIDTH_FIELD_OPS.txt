VTC Farm Monitor UI - Full Width Field Operations Readability Pass
=================================================================

Purpose
-------
The previous readability pass increased type size, but the Guided Field Validation area remained visually compressed. This pass changes the layout itself.

Changes
-------
- Guided Field Validation now breaks out to nearly the full browser viewport width.
- Resident Field Advisor primary guidance is stacked vertically instead of split into narrow columns.
- What I Am Seeing and What To Do Next are larger operational callouts.
- Why, Do Not Troubleshoot Yet, and Escalation are full-width stacked cards.
- Automatic Validation and Session Timeline are stacked instead of sharing horizontal space.
- Larger toolbar controls, workflow steps, timeline entries, and room diagnostics.
- Mobile/narrow-window fallback preserves normal contained layout.

Behavior
--------
Presentation only. No VTC Farm control, EISC, routing, SDK, CWS command, or diagnostic decision logic was changed.
